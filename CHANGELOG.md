# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Adds animated, world-anchored RGB coloring to blocks and terrain.
- Adds separate rainbow controls for sky, entities, first-person hands, items, GUI, particles, and special render effects.
- Adds an in-game settings screen, category speed and wavelength controls, and `/gaming` client commands.
- Supports client-side installation; servers do not need the mod.
